# Code of conduct

Project participation must remain professional, technical, and respectful.

Contributors are expected to:

- critique code, evidence, and design rather than people;
- preserve accurate authorship and source provenance;
- report reproducibility limits and failures without concealment;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publication of private information;
- follow University of Tehran academic-integrity requirements.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, deceptive, plagiarized, or rights-infringing contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.
